Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with us.

This is placeholder content for demonstration. Final policy should be reviewed before publishing.

1. Information we collect

When you contact Corcoran Construction through our website, by phone or by email, we may collect your name, contact details, and information about the project or enquiry you've made.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and prepare quotes;
  • Carry out and manage building work you engage us for;
  • Keep you updated on the progress of your project; and
  • Meet our legal and record-keeping obligations.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted subcontractors or suppliers only where needed to complete your project, and with parties required by law.

4. Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to protect it from loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

5. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to request access to, and correction of, the personal information we hold about you. Contact us using the details below.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, email Corcoranbuilder@gmail.com or call 027 223 0890.
